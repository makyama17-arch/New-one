Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

MAKYAMA Auto Poster is a web-based tool designed to help users prepare and publish their own video content to supported social media platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use MAKYAMA Auto Poster and how that information is handled.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the service may process:

  • Video URLs that you submit to the service.
  • Captions, titles, or other publishing information that you provide.
  • Information necessary to connect supported third-party accounts.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. Third-Party Account Authorization

MAKYAMA Auto Poster may allow you to connect supported accounts such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok through the official authorization systems provided by those platforms.

We do not ask users to provide their social-media passwords directly to MAKYAMA Auto Poster.

When you authorize a third-party platform, that platform may provide authorization information or access tokens according to the permissions you approve.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the service may be used to:

  • Provide the requested video-processing or publishing functionality.
  • Connect and manage authorized third-party accounts.
  • Maintain and secure the service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

5. Third-Party Services

MAKYAMA Auto Poster may interact with third-party services such as Google, YouTube, Meta services, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok when you choose to connect those services.

Each third-party platform has its own privacy policy, terms, and data practices. Users should review the applicable policies of those platforms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the service.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Temporary files or processing data may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to perform the requested operation, maintain the service, or resolve technical problems.

Where applicable, authorization information may be retained while a connected account remains connected to the service.

8. Account Disconnection

You may stop using a connected third-party account through the available account-management controls.

You may also revoke MAKYAMA Auto Poster's authorization through the relevant third-party platform where supported.

9. Children's Privacy

The service is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, features, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by MAKYAMA Auto Poster, please use the contact method provided by the service.
